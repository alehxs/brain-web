I am a graduated in Sports Sciences, with two master degrees in Performance Training and Sports Business. I am a rhythmic gymnast since 2007, nowadays competing at the highest level in Spain. I am also a coach since 2014, training gymnasts from 2 to 18 years old at different local and regional levels. Sport has always been my passion since I was a child, and I am working hard so it becomes my profession, trying to make the sports world evolve through science and research. I am currently focusing my work in motor variability and technical learning in sports.
