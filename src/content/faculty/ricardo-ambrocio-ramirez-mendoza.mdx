Ricardo A. Ramirez Mendoza received his Ph.D. degree in Automation and Production from Grenoble Institute of Technology, France (1997) and his MSc degree in Control from the Tec de Monterrey (1991). He has published over 500 papers in journals, conferences, etc., and he has mentored over 40 graduate students who occupy leading positions in academia&industry. His research lines include Automotive Control, Active Control, Vehicle Dynamic Control, Mechanical Vibrations, Brain-Computer-Interface, and Biomedical Signal Analysis. Has an appointment from the National System of Researchers level 2 of the Mexican government. Currently, he is Dean of Research and Graduate Studies at the School of Engineering and Sciences and Professor of Mechatronics & Mechanical Engineering.
