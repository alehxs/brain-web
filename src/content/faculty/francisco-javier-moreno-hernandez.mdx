Francis Moreno initiated his research studies in Motor Control after graduated in Granada University. His PhD study was recognized by the Special Doctorate Award, and the first research prize by the Andalusian Institute of Sports. Since then, he has conducted several projects in motor control and learning in the topics of nonlinear analysis of human movement variability and perception and action in sport.
He has been the head of the Motor Control Lab in Extremadura University and co-founder of the Motor Control Spanish Society. Currently, is the head of The Sport Research Center of Miguel Hernández University of Elche.
