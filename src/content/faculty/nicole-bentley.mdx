Dr. J. Nicole Bentley is an Associate Professor in the Department of Neurosurgery at the University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB). Her research focuses on neuromodulation therapies including deep brain stimulation for movement disorders, epilepsy surgery, and functional neurosurgery. She holds an active NIH K23 award investigating cognitive dysfunction in Parkinson's disease patients undergoing deep brain stimulation.
