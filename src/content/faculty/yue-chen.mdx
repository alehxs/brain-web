Dr. Yue Chen is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biomedical Engineering at Georgia Tech & Emory. He received his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Vanderbilt University, M.S. from Hong Kong Polytechnic University, and B.S. in Vehicle Engineering from Hunan University. His research focuses on the design, modeling, and control of continuum and soft robots for medical applications, with an emphasis on human augmentation and human-centered robotics.
