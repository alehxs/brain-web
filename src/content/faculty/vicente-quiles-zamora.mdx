Vicente Quiles has a degree in Industrial Technical Engineering specialized in Electronics (2017) by the Miguel Hernández University. He became in 2017 a Master in Artificial Intelligent from Polytechnic University of Madrid. He is currently working in the Brain-Machine Interface Systems Lab at Miguel Hernández University of Elche as a PhD student. His main interests are related to the application of new artificial intelligence techniques to decode brain signals.
