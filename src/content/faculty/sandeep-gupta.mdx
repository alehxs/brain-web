Sandeep Gupta received his doctorate from The Ohio State University in 1995. He is currently a professor and director of the School of Computing and Augmented Intelligence in the Ira A. Fulton Schools of Engineering. His areas of expertise include cyber-physical systems, green and sustainable computing, mobile and pervasive computing, and parallel and distributed computing.
