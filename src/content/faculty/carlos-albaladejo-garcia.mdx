I currently develop my work in the Motor Control Laboratory within the Sports Research Center (Miguel Hernández University), working on perceptual-visual habilities and cognitive processes and their relation with sport performance. I am carrying out methodological procedures regarding to visual search strategies using eye-tracking glasses and inhibitory control parameters registration systems.
