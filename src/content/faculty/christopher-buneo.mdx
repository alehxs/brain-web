Associate Professor Chris Buneo joined Arizona State University's Schools of Biological and Health Systems Engineering in 2005 after completing postdoctoral and senior research fellowships at Caltech. Chris received his Ph.D. in Physiology at the University of Minnesota and a BS-MS in Physical Therapy from Long Island University. His research focuses on sensorimotor control, neurophysiology, and neuromodulation.
