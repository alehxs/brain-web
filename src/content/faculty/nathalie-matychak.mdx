Nathalie Matychak is the Assistant Director of Producing and Residency at Georgia Tech Arts. Originally from Brooklyn, NY, she brings over a decade of experience in the performing arts field. She previously served as Booking Associate for the Southern region at Pentacle, an arts management nonprofit, where she represented a diverse roster of dance and theater companies. She has created her own dance works for stage and screen since 2010 and co-founded an emerging female choreographers project in 2013. She holds a BFA in Dance Performance & Choreography from NYU Tisch School of the Arts.
