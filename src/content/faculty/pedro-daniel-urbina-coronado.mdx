Pedro Daniel Urbina Coronado obtained his Ph.D. degree in Engineering Science from Tecnologico de Monterrey, Monterrey México, in 2014. From 2015 to 2017 he worked as a postdoc at Tecnologico de Monterrey first and after that in the School of Mechanical Engineering at Georgia Institute of Technology. He is been working as a professor of the Department of Mechanical Engineering since 2018. His primary areas of interest are a cyber-physical system, augmented reality for education and industrial application as well as mechanical design. Dr. Urbina is a member of the Mexican National Academy of Researchers (SNI – I).
