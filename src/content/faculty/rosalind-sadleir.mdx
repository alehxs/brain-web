Rosalind (Ros) Sadleir is an associate professor in the School of Biological and Health Systems Engineering at Arizona State University. She is the head of the Neuro-electricity Laboratory, which is concerned with modeling and imaging biological conditions using targeted electrical methods. Her team's work varies from the very practical (including device design and commercial development) to the conceptual and theoretical.
