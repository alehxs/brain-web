My area of specialization in research is BIOMECHANICS OF HUMAN MOVEMENT and MOTOR CONTROL. More specifically, my research experience is oriented to the analysis of human motor variability and stability. I'm also interested in researching wearable technology to quantify the quality of body movement. Finally, my last research focus is to analyze the effectiveness of physical training to improve the gait and balance of people with multiple sclerosis.
