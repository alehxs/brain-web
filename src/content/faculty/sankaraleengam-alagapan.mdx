Dr. Sankaraleengam Alagapan is a Research Scientist in the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Georgia Tech and the Institute for Neuroscience, Neurotechnology, and Society. His work focuses on understanding neural circuits underlying cognition and developing brain stimulation approaches for treating neural disorders, combining computational neuroscience, neural engineering, and neuro AI methods.
