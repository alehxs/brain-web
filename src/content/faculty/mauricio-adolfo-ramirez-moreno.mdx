Dr. Mauricio Ramírez received his Bachellor in Mechatronics Engineering in 2010,  and MS and PhD in Biomedican Engineering in 2015 and 2019 in Cinvestav Monterrey (México).  In 2019, he started teaching university level courses in the Biomedical and Mechatronics Engineering Programs at Tecnológico de Monterrey (México) and performed collaborative research activities with students and professors. Since the summer of 2020 he is a postdoc at BRAIN Monterrey Site and he also serves as program manager, coordinating research projects with students and industry members. His main research interests include Brain Computer Interfaces, neuroengineering, robotics, biomechanics and machine learning.
