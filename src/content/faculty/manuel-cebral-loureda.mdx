Dr. Manuel Cebral-Loureda is a Research Professor in Digital Humanities at the School of Humanities and Education, Tecnológico de Monterrey (Campus Monterrey). He holds a doctorate in Philosophy along with master's degrees in Art, Philosophy and Creativity and in Statistical Learning and Data Mining. Before joining Tecnológico de Monterrey, he worked as a lecturer in Spain and gained experience in cultural production and educational innovation at the intersections of art and technology. He is the author of "Teoria da imagem" (Axóuxere Editora, 2018) and has published widely on posthumanism, neurohumanities, and digital methods in the humanities.
