I am a Doctor of Philosophy and have a master's degree in "Art, Philosophy and Creativity" and another in "Statistical Learning and Data Mining". In addition to having worked as a teacher in Spain, I have experience in cultural production and management with projects linked to educational innovation at the intersections of art and technology. I have published the book "Teoria da imagem" with Axóuxere Editora (2018), as well as several research articles in recent years. Currently my areas of interest are focused on digital Humanities, through programming language and data management; as well as in the issues surrounding Posthumanism, as a relationship between humans and technology and the new challenges of the s. XXI.
