Tomás Urbán holds a degree in Sport Science and a PhD in Sport Science from the Miguel Hernández University of Elche (UMH). He also has a different Master’s degree in Sports Performance and Health and Education.
Currently, he works as an Associate Professor and Technical support in teaching and research in the Sport Research Center (CID) at the same university.
His main research interest is to know the functional role of motor variability in order to improve motor control and learning process, as well as knowing the most effective training methods and effects to improve the learning process of sports technique.
